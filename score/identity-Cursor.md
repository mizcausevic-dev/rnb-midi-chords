# Study identity

Container lengths, tempos, and collections were fixed before note generation. No stochastic draws.

## s01 Pocket

Each chord in variation A enters on the downbeat and leaves beat 4 empty. Variation B waits until beat 2 and spreads the chord into three notes a fourth, fifth, or sixth apart.

Limitations: Bass A fits variation A. Bass B fits variation B. The empty beat is space, not a missing chord.

## s02 Borrowed

Variation A starts every chord on beat 2 and holds it for two beats, so the downbeat is a rest. Variation B starts on the downbeat. On the two borrowed bars it plays the Ab chord for one beat, then E G C.

Limitations: The borrowed chords are the color. Do not treat Ab major as the tonic.

## s03 Pedal

After bar 1, each chord arrives a sixteenth early. Variation B stays on the downbeat and thins to two notes.

Limitations: The early attack ends before the next early attack. Nothing starts before tick 0.

## s04 Two Register

Two attacks in every bar, on beat 1 and beat 3. Variation B swaps them: the color comes first and the shell answers.

Limitations: The two attacks are one progression, not two songs. Use one bass file with one keys variation.

## s05 Plagal

Each chord in variation A is held for two bars. Variation B uses one bar of the chord, then one bar on a single tone: G, Gb, F, then D.

Limitations: Gb is outside Bb major and is flagged. Variation A uses it only in the Ebm bars. Variation B also holds that Gb alone on bar 4, before Ebm. It is not a new key.

## s06 Late Sus

Variation A starts every chord on the second eighth and holds it for three beats. Variation B moves the same harmony onto beat 4 as a short stab.

Limitations: The late entrance is the pocket. Do not move these chords to the downbeat and call it the same study.

## s07 Space

A chord only on the odd bars, held for that one bar. Variation B fills the even bars with a single whole-note guide tone.

Limitations: The rests are written. They are not a loop that failed to copy.

## s08 No Tonic

Variation A plays one note at a time, a quarter each. Variation B stacks the three shell tones and holds them.

Limitations: There is no tonic to resolve to. The last A7 is the end of the phrase, not a mistake.
